Privacy Policy

Last updated: September 23, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how the VNM Bot ("the bot") handles information when it is used in a Discord server. The bot is operated by VNM ("we", "us"). If you have questions or requests, contact us at vnmbusiness@gmail.com.

Information the bot accesses

To provide its features, the bot accesses the following data from Discord:

  • Account identifiers — Discord user IDs, usernames, and avatars.
  • Server & role data — the members of a server and their roles, so the bot can perform role and channel management.
  • Commands and inputs — the bot works through Discord slash commands, direct messages, and messages that explicitly mention or reply to the bot. It processes the command text or requested follow-up value so it can perform the requested action.
  • Tournament archive content — when an authorized server administrator explicitly confirms tournament teardown or creator offboarding, the bot reads messages, embeds, reactions, and attachments from the temporary tournament channels being removed so it can create an administrative event archive.
  • Administrative message audits — a user with Discord's Manage Messages permission may explicitly request a limited recent-message view from a channel that user is already permitted to view. The bot checks the user's channel permissions before returning any content.
  • Voice channel presence — which voice channels have members and how many, used for voice-channel tracking.
  • Direct messages it sends — the bot may send direct messages (for example, notifications to team captains).
  • Direct-message support — DMs that a user sends to the bot, including follow-up replies and attachments, are copied to a designated staff-only Discord channel so authorized VNM staff can provide support. Staff replies sent through that inbox are delivered to the user by the bot.

What we store, and where

  • Operational logs — the bot keeps logs on its host machine recording actions taken and the Discord user ID/username involved, for debugging and abuse prevention. These logs are stored securely and are deleted when no longer needed; you may request deletion at any time.
  • Google Sheets operations — voice tracking writes aggregate channel names and member counts. When authorized staff explicitly use roster, role-export, code-delivery, or event-sync features, the bot may also read or write Discord display names, usernames, role names, event assignments, and operation timestamps in the staff-selected spreadsheet.
  • Tournament / event configuration — settings for tournaments and events created through the bot are stored as local configuration files on the host.
  • Tournament archives (Google Drive) — after an authorized administrator confirms teardown or offboarding, the bot stores a transcript and copies of attachments from the affected temporary event channels in a private Google Drive archive controlled by VNM. The bot does not archive unrelated server channels.
  • DM support records (Discord) — incoming bot DMs and staff replies are retained in VNM's designated staff-only Discord support channel until staff delete them or the channel's retention practices remove them.
  • Game statistics — when a stats command is used, player statistics are fetched live from a third-party game-stats service and shown in Discord. The bot does not build or keep player profiles.

The bot does not collect payment information and does not sell data or use it for advertising. Direct messages sent to the bot are handled as described above for commands and staff support.

Third-party services

The bot relies on a small number of services to function:

  • Discord — the platform the bot operates on, governed by Discord's own Privacy Policy.
  • Google (Sheets & Drive) — used to record aggregate voice-channel data and to export tournament files.
  • A third-party game-stats provider — queried live when a stats command is used.

Data retention

Operational logs are retained on the host machine for debugging and abuse prevention and are deleted when no longer needed. Voice-channel data in Google Sheets is overwritten on each update; other staff-requested spreadsheet data is retained until the spreadsheet owner deletes or replaces it. Active tournament/event configuration is kept only as long as it is needed to run the relevant event. Tournament archives are retained in VNM's private Google Drive for administrative event records until the event organizer requests deletion or VNM determines the archive is no longer needed. You may request deletion of your data at any time (see below).

Your choices and deletion requests

You can request that we delete data associated with you or your server—including content contained in a tournament archive—or ask a question about your data, by emailing vnmbusiness@gmail.com. Removing the bot from a server stops all further data access for that server.

Children

The bot is not directed to children. Use of Discord and the bot is subject to Discord's minimum age requirements.

Changes to this policy

We may update this Privacy Policy from time to time. When we do, we will revise the "Last updated" date at the top of this page.
